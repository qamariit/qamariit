## 👋 About me

I build mobile apps end to end: product idea, UI, backend integration, App Store release, and the updates that follow. Most of my work has been **iOS and Flutter apps for FinTech and consumer products**, including payments, transactions, onboarding and retention features.

- 📱 **Shipping since 2019:** my own apps on the App Store, plus client and company products
- 🏦 **FinTech experience:** payment flows, transaction screens, secure auth, and the edge cases that come with money
- 📊 **Now adding data skills:** Python, SQL, Power BI and simple machine learning, with projects at [github.com/qib-fast](https://github.com/qib-fast)
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Swift, SwiftUI, Flutter, Firebase, App Store review, in-app purchases

## 🔍 How I work

1. **Ship small, ship often.** A working build on a device beats a perfect plan.
2. **Own the whole thing.** Design, code, backend, store listing, reviews, updates.
3. **Measure what shipped.** Crashes, retention, conversions. The data side of my work grew out of this.
4. **Write it down.** Readable code, short READMEs, and commit messages a future me can follow.
